What the index score is made of

Four parts, weighted. Nothing is a guess dressed up as a number — where a figure is missing, the card says so instead of filling the gap.

01 · WEIGHT 50%

50%

Degree strength

A published subject score for that university in that degree — mostly THE World University Rankings 2026 by subject. A school missing from the table sits below the ranked ones rather than being invented a score.

02 · WEIGHT 20%

20%

Admission fit

Your average against the published cutoff, as a curve rather than a cliff. Results are labelled likely admit, match or reach — a cutoff is a published minimum, not a prediction of your odds.

03 · WEIGHT 15% + 15%

30%

Cost and preferences

Tuition acts as a hard filter; cost fit then nudges gently inside it. Preferences — co-op, city size, crime — only count when you ask for them.

“Scores weigh admission fit, all-in cost, and the preferences you set. Costs are ranges, not single figures, and every figure carries its source and a check-by date.{% if weights.source != 'learned' %} Ranking weights are currently hand-set rather than fitted to data.{% endif %}”
